Our story

A UK family business since 2011

Natural Juices & Vitamins Ltd started in 2011 with a simple idea: make high-quality, naturally sourced supplements easy to buy, with the kind of friendly service you get from a family business.

Our own LoveNoni, LoveTart Cherry, Love Mangosteen and Love Goji juices are organic certified (GB-ORG-04) and bottled in glass. Alongside them we stock carefully chosen brands including BetterYou, Natroceutics, Coyne Healthcare, Dynamic Health and Blooming Blends.

We are based in Sunbury-on-Thames, and our team answers the phone six days a week, 9am–8pm. Every UK order ships free, and orders placed before 2pm are usually despatched the same working day.

Why customers stay with us
