Our promise to the planet

Good for you, kinder to the earth

A supplement is only truly healthy if it is healthy for the planet too.

Naturally sourced, always

At Natural Juices, we believe better wellness starts with better ingredients. That means choosing real plants, fruits and botanicals wherever possible, and keeping our products connected to the natural sources they come from. From aloe and noni to cherries, berries and botanical extracts, our focus is on ingredients that feel familiar, purposeful and easy to trust.

Why sourcing matters

A product is only as good as what goes into it. Naturally sourced ingredients help us keep our range closer to nature, while supporting a cleaner and more transparent approach to wellness. We want customers to feel confident that they are choosing products made with care, not unnecessary complexity.

Plant-based ingredients with purpose

Every ingredient should have a reason for being there. We are interested in ingredients with a history of use, strong customer interest and a clear place in everyday wellness routines. This means avoiding the idea of adding things just because they sound impressive. Simple, recognisable and purposeful will always feel better than overcomplicated.

Quality over shortcuts

Natural sourcing is not just about where an ingredient comes from. It is also about how carefully it is selected, handled and presented. We aim to work with products that meet clear quality expectations and give customers confidence in what they are buying. Wellness should feel honest, not confusing.

Our ongoing commitment

We know sustainability is not a one-time claim. It is a direction. We are continuing to look for better sourcing options, more responsible suppliers and clearer ways to communicate what is inside our products. Small improvements matter. Better choices add up.

The takeaway: Naturally sourced ingredients are at the heart of what we do. Our goal is simple: offer wellness products that feel cleaner, more transparent and closer to nature, without making things complicated.

BPA & PFAS free

Clean wellness should not stop at the product inside the bottle. Packaging matters too. That is why we care about reducing exposure to unwanted packaging chemicals such as BPA and PFAS, often referred to as “forever chemicals.” Our aim is to offer products that feel cleaner from the inside out — from the ingredients we choose to the packaging standards we work towards.

Why this matters

Customers are paying more attention to what they put into their bodies, but packaging is part of that conversation too. It is not enough for a product to look healthy on the shelf. It should be held to a responsible standard at every stage. BPA and PFAS have become important topics because people want packaging that is safer, cleaner and more future-conscious.

A cleaner packaging approach

We want our customers to feel confident when choosing Natural Juices. That means prioritising packaging choices that support product quality while avoiding materials and chemicals that do not align with our values. Where possible, we aim to work with packaging that is free from BPA and PFAS, while continuing to improve as better options become available.

Better for people, better for trust

Sustainability is not only about the planet. It is also about trust. Customers should not have to second-guess whether a wellness product has been packaged responsibly. Clearer standards help create a better experience from the moment someone picks up the product.

Our ongoing commitment

Packaging technology is always changing, and we see that as a good thing. We will continue reviewing our packaging choices, supplier information and material standards so we can keep improving over time. Progress matters more than pretending everything is perfect.

The takeaway: Our BPA & PFAS free approach reflects a simple belief: wellness products should be clean, considered and responsible — not just in what they contain, but in how they are packaged too.

Lower-carbon packaging

Sustainability is not a finish line. It is a series of better choices made again and again. For us, packaging is one of the most important areas where small changes can make a real difference. We are working towards packaging that is more efficient, less wasteful and lighter on the planet.

Why packaging matters

Every bottle, label, box and delivery choice has an impact. While packaging protects the product and keeps it safe for customers, it should also be designed with responsibility in mind. That means asking better questions: Can we reduce unnecessary materials? Can we use recyclable options? Can we make packaging lighter without compromising quality?

Reducing what we do not need

One of the simplest ways to lower impact is to avoid over-packaging. We want our packaging to feel premium and trustworthy, but not excessive. Good packaging should protect the product, explain it clearly and arrive safely — without creating waste for the sake of appearance.

Moving towards recyclable materials

Where possible, we are looking to use recyclable and more responsible packaging options. This includes reviewing bottles, caps, labels and outer packaging so we can make improvements across the full customer journey. It is not just about one part of the product. It is about the whole process from storage to delivery.

Practical progress, not empty promises

We know customers are tired of vague sustainability claims. So our approach is simple: keep improving, be honest about the journey and make practical changes that actually matter. Lower-carbon packaging is not about sounding perfect. It is about doing better, step by step.

The takeaway: Our goal is to reduce waste, improve recyclability and make packaging choices that are kinder to the planet. Small bottles, big intentions — and a commitment to keep moving in the right direction.
