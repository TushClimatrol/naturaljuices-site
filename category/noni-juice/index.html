About our noni juice

Noni (Morinda citrifolia) is a tropical fruit that has been used in Pacific Island and Southeast Asian food traditions for centuries. It has a famously strong, distinctive flavour, which is why most people take it as a small daily measure of juice rather than eating the fruit.

Our LoveNoni juices are organic certified (GB-ORG-04) and bottled in glass, which avoids plastic contact with the juice. If the classic taste isn't for you, the raspberry blend gives you the same noni with a smoother, fruitier finish.
