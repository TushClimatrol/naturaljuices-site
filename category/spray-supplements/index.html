About our oral sprays

Oral sprays deliver a measured dose of a vitamin or mineral that you spray into your mouth — no water and no tablets. BetterYou's range covers vitamin D (DLux 1000 and 3000), B12, iron, multivitamins, vitamin K2 and vitamin C.

The magnesium range is applied to the skin instead: magnesium oil sprays, gel, lotions and bath flakes made with pure magnesium chloride. They are a popular choice for people who would rather not take magnesium by mouth.
